[Heading2] Contracts
[] Warlords are acquired through the opening of contracts. Contracts will have specific drop rates for warlords depending on the quality of the contract opened. A whole contract can be gained from various areas of the game as well as the breaking down of existing warlords at a rate of 1-2 scrap pieces per warlord. 10 scraps will make a whole contract.

[Heading3] Player Level
[] As the player progresses through the games ‘Jobs’ and ‘Life Goals’ they will earn XP. They will also earn 1XP from each energy they spend. Each level will give the player a maximum energy refill and provide additional rewards such as Credits, Contracts and Trinium.

[Heading3] Pit Rank (PVP)
[] As outlined in the Pit section, there are 13 Ranks to the PVP side of the game. With two PVP aspects to the game there are two sets of ranks both of which will offer a regular weekly cache as a reward. Any and all players can ascend up the ranks to the top with the caveat that losses on offence and defense lose them points. Ranks 1-4 lose 10 pints, 5-8 lose 15 points, 9-12 lose 20 points and rank 13 loses 25 points. Victories each day from offensive fights will gain points +10 each.
