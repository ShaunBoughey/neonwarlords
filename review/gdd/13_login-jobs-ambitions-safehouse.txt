[Heading3] Monthly Login Rewards
[] The monthly login rewards will be set to begin with, releasing a specified Epic Warlord for everyone to get. After so many months it’ll then become a rolling reward. This reward table will only progress on login. If a player does not login for a day there is no reset or deletion of progress. An additional feature would be to be able to randomise the groupings, EG month 1 is energy on yellow, month 2 is XP or credits on yellow etc.
