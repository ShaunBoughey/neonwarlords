[Heading2] Warlord Class
[] All Warlords split into classes with each representing their general roles and the stats they most care about being increased. Though the classes have a general desired role they are not limited to it. The control of the warlord’s balance will be strongly rooted in their base stats.  I.E. A ‘Armour’ Warlord will generally have a decent defensive stats but may still hit hard with a high ability multiplier. A Warlord with 1000 base armour receives a bigger scaling from equipment than a warlord with 900 armour.
[] 
[] Class
[] Role
[] Armour
[] Tank: Dmg is scaled with the Armour stat. Focuses on taking punishment.
[] Firepower
[] DPS: High multipliers on abilities combined with damage being scaled off of the Firepower stat. Focuses on hitting the hardest. 
[] Modder
[] Buffer: Dmg scaled with the Health stat. Focuses on a supporting role, increasing speeds and team strengths.
[] Hacker
[] Debuffer:  Dmg is scaled with the Hacking stat. Focuses on a debilitating role of hindering the other team often placing various debuffs.

[Heading2] Warlord Syndicate
[] Each warlord has a ‘Syndicate’ tag attached to them to represent the group that they derive from. Some Warlords will use abilities that call on the tag to work. I.E ‘This warlord attacks and calls two other  ‘X Syndicate’ warlords to attack the same target’. If the team does not contain other ‘X Syndicate’ tagged warlords, no additional attacks will be called.
