[Heading3] Hideout: Heist (Money)
[] The Heist is a Credit gain focused location with the primary reward being large sums of credits. Additional Contracts can be earned a rare drop as well as the occasional huge payout.
[] The Heist Hideout is a limited entry location requiring a Heist Plan in order to participate. These will be refreshed to the players account at regular intervals with the player receiving 5 plans a day. The total is capped at 5 so any not used in a day will be lost. This is another incentive to participate daily.
