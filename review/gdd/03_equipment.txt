[Heading2] Warlord Equipment
[Heading3] Gear
[] There will be six slots per warlord; Weapon, Boots, Legs, Chest, Helmet and Gloves. These are accessible with any level warlord.  
[Heading3] Implants
[] There are also three Implant slots; Limbs, Organs, Skeleton. These will be unlocked based on your Reputation rank. Unlocking Limbs at rank 2, Organs at rank 4 and Skeleton at rank 6.
[Heading3] Neons
[] Neons are additional upgrade materials that attach to equipment, improving a substat by its corresponding designation. Neons can only be applied to Equipment of the same or higher rank.  Neons drop from ‘Syndicate Wars’ and range from rank 1-6 and cover all substats except Critical Chance.
[] 
[] Example: A Rank 5 Speed Neon will increase a speed substat of an equipment piece ranked 5 or 6. It cannot be applied to Equipment without a speed substat or or rank =&lt;4
[Heading3] Equipment Leveling
[] All equipment contains a primary stat that increases with each level and sub stats that are either awarded or increased at 5 level intervals. Credits can be used to level your equipment  from 0 - 20 gaining and additional substat roll every 5 levels. Equipment is also inherently ranked by quality when acquired ranging from ranks 1 - 6. Equipment rank does not change once acquired.

[Heading3] Equipment Acquisition
[] Equipment’s main avenue of acquisition will be through the various Hideouts, each rendering up a different selection available to farm, though these will not be the exclusive locations. Campaign will also drop selected sets at a lower quality and rank, the game game shops will sell it for Credits and Premium currency and it will be rewarded for rank standing in PVP content.
