[Heading2] Campaign
[] The full campaign in Neon Warlords is split into 10 Locations each containing 7 Stages. Stages 1 - 6 will each reward some credits, xp, chance to drop a Warlord of Rarity common-rare and equipment of a specified set, Other than Location 10 which will rotate its reward set daily. Each stage contains requirements to be completed to 3 Chevrons which as accumulated throughout the campaign will result in rewards. Once the first campaign is completed, a new difficulty will open up, beginning the process again with a total of 3 difficulties overall. The XP gained from each battle should divide equally amongst the 5 Warlords used.
