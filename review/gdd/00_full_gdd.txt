[Heading1] Game Analysis
[] Neon Warlords is a mobile game primarily aimed at people who love to level up characters as a form of progression and manipulate statistics to improve their characters for the purpose of PvE(Campaign, Hideouts) and PvP(Arena fights).
[] This is in the same vein as RAID, SWGOH and Overhit. The design itself will be 3D harnessing the power of Unreal Engine with a clean GUI. 
[Heading1] Mission Statement
[] In Neon Warlords, you start as a ragtag group who has to fight their way through the gangs of the city, in doing so leveling up your characters, collecting new gear and finding new companions along the way. The game will include PvE in the form of a campaign and boss fights in hideouts. There will also be a PvP aspect where you are able to fight other players' teams!  This will be an Android game with the aspiration to support IOS at a later date.
[Heading1] Genre
[] Mobile looter, collection, PvE, PvP

[Heading1] Target Audience
[] This game is targeted towards gamers who enjoy a grindy looter game that allows for a lot of progression with your characters. This should be someone who enjoys spending small amounts of time, regularly running missing or possibly with a side of being able to idle the game. The target audience often dislike energy limits and duplication of gear from missions. There is often a desire to be in the top percentile of players as a ‘bragging right’, it gives players a form of satisfaction to be higher than others. This Also constantly gives others something to aim for.

[Heading3] Leveling Methods
[] Warlords can be leveled through two methods. The first is taking them into a battle and receiving exp upon each completion which costs energy and time. The second is to take them into the Dojo and use varying levels of ‘Metabolics’ which provide Exp. This Exp bonus is higher if you align the right metabolic with the Warlord’s reputation. 
[Heading2] Warlord Neuronet (Skilltree)
[] The Warlord’s neuronet is split into 7 ‘synapse layers’ acting as the progression levels and 3 ‘careers’ acting as the class trees; Assault, Protection and Control.
[] Acquiring memory fragments will improve a warlord’s skill tree and skills increase in cost as the tree progresses. Fragments are split into tier 1, 2 and 3 with later stages of the associated hideout producing more rewards of all three types. Warlords can assign 4 full memory slots per Synapse layer with most skills allowing for two points each, however they may only select 1 of the 2 presented in each career.  This should allow for diverse skill selection to benefit the inherent abilities of the selected Warlord.

[Heading2] Warlord Abilities
[] Abilities can be level through multiple methods. One method is the consuming of a duplicate character to level the skill by 3 levels. Alternatively abilities can be raised 1 level through the use of the appropriate ‘Stimm’. The rarity of the warlord will require a stimm of equal rarity or higher.  Warlords ability count will vary from warlord to warlord giving them individual requirements. This will include a mixture of active, gang and passive abilities. All abilities have their own unique damage multiplies to help balance their damage output with their scaled states. Ability Damage scales off of both the ability multiplier and the appropriate stat.
[] Active Abilities to a max of 3: Require activation during the combat.
[] Passive Abilities: Always passively active during the combat.
[] Leader/Syndicate Abilities to a max of 1: Only active when leading the team. Provides a passive bonus.
[] Reinforce Abilities to a max of 2: Will only trigger if the Character reinforces during Pit Royal content or at the start of a combat round.
[] Ability Example
[] Bob:
[] Ab 1 - Attack 1 enemy Reduce their tm by 10% apply SPEED UP for one turn on self
[] Ab 2 - Attack all enemies Reduce Enemy tm by 10% and a 50% chance to apply ARMOUR DOWN on all enemies for two turns. Cooldown of 3 turns.

[Heading3] Gear

[] There are also three Implant slots; Limbs, Organs, Skeleton. These will be unlocked based on your Reputation rank. Unlocking Limbs at rank 2, Organs at rank 4 and Skeleton at rank 6.

[] Neons are additional upgrade materials that attach to equipment, improving a substat by its corresponding designation. Neons can only be applied to Equipment of the same or higher rank.  Neons drop from ‘Syndicate Wars’ and range from rank 1-6 and cover all substats except Critical Chance.
[] Example: A Rank 5 Speed Neon will increase a speed substat of an equipment piece ranked 5 or 6. It cannot be applied to Equipment without a speed substat or or rank =<4

[Heading2] Warlord Class
[] All Warlords split into classes with each representing their general roles and the stats they most care about being increased. Though the classes have a general desired role they are not limited to it. The control of the warlord’s balance will be strongly rooted in their base stats.  I.E. A ‘Armour’ Warlord will generally have a decent defensive stats but may still hit hard with a high ability multiplier. A Warlord with 1000 base armour receives a bigger scaling from equipment than a warlord with 900 armour.
<TABLE>

Firepower | DPS: High multipliers on abilities combined with damage being scaled off of the Firepower stat. Focuses on hitting the hardest.

Hacker | Debuffer:  Dmg is scaled with the Hacking stat. Focuses on a debilitating role of hindering the other team often placing various debuffs.
</TABLE>

[Heading1] Combat
[Heading2] Flow of Combat
[] Start fight button. Match loads to show both teams ready and facing eachother. 
[] All turn meters start. This is almost instantaneous stopping when the first warlord gains enough turn meter to fill their bar entirely.
[] Take the warlords turn by first selecting which ability to use.
[] Then select the target to use the ability on. Targets depend on the ability being used.
[] Ability activates and applies all relative effects of the ability; turn meter loss or gain, dmg to targets, buff/debuffs etc.
[] Trigger all relevant passive effects due to events and resolve their effects. Most passives should not then trigger additional passives.

[Heading2] Total Damage Equation
[] The total damage equation should factor in all forms of damage multipliers making a reliance on certain balance control values. A Warlord ability multiplier will be static and will aid in control by being the first number the total stats are multiplied by. 
[] Total Damage =  Total Base Stats * Warlord Ability Multiplier * Ability Level Multipliers * Crit Damage Multiplier *  Neuronet Bonuses * Currently Applied Buffs * Passive Effects
[] The damage mitigation percentage is taken from the total damage at the end to establish a final damage number on the attacked target.
[Heading2] Total Damage Mitigation Equation
[] The total damage mitigation will work out total armour value after all buffs and multipliers are applied, once again keeping a control on warlord balance through a warlord’s base stat values. This will be converted to a mitigation percentage which will then be applied to the damage being dealt. 
[] Damage Mitigation = Total Base Armour * Neuronet Bonuses * Currently Applied Buffs * Passive Bonuses = Total Damage Mitigation Percentage.
[] Finally the Armour penetration values is taken from the Total Damage Mitigation Percentage

[Heading3] Example Equation
[] A Warlord has a total base stat of 1000 Firepowers, Ability multiplier of 2.0, Ability Level Multiplier of 10%, 50% Critical Damage, Neuronet Bonuses of 15%, 30% Firepower Buff Currently Applied, A passive of 10% firepower and a 5% armour penetration.
[] 1000*2.0*1.1*1.5*1.15*1.3*1.1 = 5437 Total Damage
[] A Warlord has a total base stat 1000 Armour, Neuro Net Bonus of 20% Armour, 15% Armour Buff Currently Applied, and a 10% Passive. 
[] 1000*1.2*1.15*1.1 = 1518 Armour = 50% Damage Mitigation
[] Final Damage is calculated using one against the other whilst taking into account Armour Penetration.
[] 5437 - 45% (50%Armour - 5% Armour Penetration) = 2990 Damage dealt to the Warlord’s HealthPool.
[Heading2] Hacking Vs Firewall
[] Whenever a warlord uses a skill that attempts to debuff or manipulate the turnmeter of an enemy Warlord a Hacking vs Firewall check will be made to assess if the buff will land.

[] Buffing: Check if Debuff % is successful > Check for Blocking Buffs Status > Check if Target already has the Relevant Debuff > Can Buff be Refreshed > Place/Replace Relevant Buff for Relevant Turns.

[Heading2] Hideouts
[] Hideouts are set challenges often centered around specific mechanics that must be overcome with the deployed team in order to obtain victory. I.E two waves of strong enemies followed by a boss who cannot be killed unless both of his accompanying minions are dead, with the minions factoring some form of revive mechanic. The goal being to overcome the revive mechanics by a block revive skill or killing them all at the correctimes. This creates a dynamic economy amongst the Warlord viability as certain Warlords will excel within certain Hideouts. These Hideouts will be the number one location to obtain high rank and rarity equipment, increasing the reward payout chances through stages 1-20.

His two trusted acolytes will also Buff Deacon whilst dealing heavy damage to debuffed Warlords. [Heading2] The Pit
[Heading3] Pit Brawls
[] Pit Brawls is the Player Vs Player aspect of the game (PVP). Brawl Invites are required to enter the brawls and are capped at 10. Refills of Brawl Invites are refreshed daily, can be earned through missions and can be purchased with Trinium. All players assign 5 Warlords as a Defensive Gang. When players select to fight in the pit, they are presented with a selection of 10 defensive gangs from players within their ‘Skill Bracket’ and ‘Ranking’. They may then tailor their ‘Offensive’ team to battle against the defensive gang. If the player is victorious they will gain Pit Coin (PTC used with the Safehouse and black market) and PVP Ranking. At the end of each 1 week Ranking period the player will receive rewards based on their ‘Skill Bracket’. A player can ascend brackets at any time as they acquire more rankings. If a player's defensive gang is beaten, they will lose PVP ranking allowing them to descend skill brackets over time.
[] Global Leaderboards will show the player’s defensive teams, weekly earned rankings and bracket as well as filter to ‘gang’, ‘friends’ and ‘search for players’.  
[Heading3] Pit Royal
[] This is an extended version of Pit Brawls aimed at players with more bloated rosters. Royal Invites are required to enter and are capped at 10. Refills are earned daily and through missions as well as able to be purchased with Trinium. A player instead picks a team of 8 Warlords, 5 for the initially deployed gang and 3 as reinforcements. A reinforcement will enter the fight when one member of the gang dies taking their slot. Due to this mechanic NO REVIVES will be allowed during this mode. Warlords with Reinforce abilities become great for this mode as they will provide a bonus on entering the fight, helping to sway the tide of battle.

[Heading2] Battle Pass 
[] The battle pass is a seasonal reward track that lasts for 10 weeks with a  2 week gap. During this time the players can level their pass from 1-100 gaming rewards along the way.  There will be two tracks, Free and Premium. Free players will only be able to receive the rewards aligned with the free track, whilst Premium players will receive the free track as well as the bonus rewards aligned in the premium track. Optionally you can purchase a Boosted Premium Pass which will boot the players pass straight to level 25. Reducing the amount they have to do to complete the pass during that season. 
[] The pass is leveled through the completion of daily and weekly challenges which each reward the player with ‘Merits’. Each level of the pass requires 20 Merits progress. Additionally, each week during the season will add a set of ‘Weekly’ challenges which offer up a total of 100 Merits to further help boost progress. The challenges will randomly select from an assortment and players have the ability to reset 1 weekly challenge from each of the respective weeks.
