NEON WARLORDS - PC build
========================

Run NeonWarlords.exe. Keep NeonWarlords.pck in the same folder (it holds the game).

Saves live in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Neon Warlords\neon_warlords_save.json
Settings (in game): battle speed, auto battle, music, sound effects, fullscreen,
Free IAP dev mode, and "Developer: grant test items" for a fully stocked account.

Dev build: every new account also gets Deacon Chainz, the first warlord with a finished 3D model
(put him in your squad to see him in battle). Other character art and models are placeholders; music and sound effects
are synthesised.
